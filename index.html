Attendance by camera, not by roll call.

EduVision recognises registered students on the classroom camera, shows how many people are in the room, and keeps every record in one place for your school.

See what it does

One record of who was in school, and when.

EduVision replaces the paper register with a camera, and gives admins, teachers and reviewers each the tools they need.

  • Smart Attendance

    Register a student's face once. After that, the classroom camera recognises them and marks them present.

  • Smart Occupancy

    A live headcount from the camera, so you always know how many people are in the room.

  • Students and teachers

    School admins manage student and teacher accounts. Teachers get a focused view for attendance and reports.

  • Reports

    Search attendance by student, class, date or status, then export the results as a CSV file.

  • Reviewed school access

    New schools register for access and are approved by EduVision before they go live.

How a class takes attendance

  1. 1

    Register

    A school admin adds each student and registers a face profile. The app asks them to confirm that permission was given.

  2. 2

    Recognise

    A teacher opens Smart Attendance and keeps one registered student in front of the camera. Matching runs in the browser.

  3. 3

    Record

    Attendance is saved to the school's records, ready to search and export from Reports.

Face profiles need consent.

Registering a student means using camera frames to calculate a recognition profile. EduVision asks for a confirmation of school or guardian permission before it will register anyone.

Removing a student also removes their attendance records and face profile.
